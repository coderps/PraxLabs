# Privacy Policy for Baby Tracker

Effective date: 19 November 2026
Developer: Prax Labs, contact: labsprax@gmail.com
Package: com.praxlabs.babytracker

Baby Tracker is a diary for the parents of a baby. It records what you log (feeds, sleep, crying, moods,
weights, heights, events, the baby's name and birth date) and calculates estimates from it.

## What we collect
We, the developer, do not collect your data. The app has no account, no advertising, no analytics of ours, no crash
reporting of ours and no server of ours. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

One component inside the app is made by Google: ML Kit barcode scanning, which reads the QR codes of a day image you
import. Google describes this component as collecting technical diagnostics: device model and OS version, the app's
package name and version, a per-installation identifier that is not meant to identify you, performance measurements and
error codes, sent to Google over HTTPS (see Google's "ML Kit Android data disclosure" page). We do not receive this data,
and it does not contain what you log about your baby.

## Where your data is
- On your phone, in the app's private storage. Deleting the app deletes it.
- If you pair a second phone, the two phones exchange the data directly over your local Wi-Fi. No server is involved.
  The link is authenticated with a shared secret that only the paired phones know. It is not encrypted, so use it on a
  network you trust.
- Backup files, CSV files, PDF reports and day images are created only when you ask. You choose where they go
  (Downloads or the share sheet). Once you share a file, the receiving app or person controls it.
- The Android system may back up the app's data to your Google account if you have Android backup turned on. That is
  controlled by your phone settings and Google's policies.
- To find your partner's phone, the app tries the other addresses of your own Wi-Fi network on port 8765. This stays on
  your local network.

## Sharing
We do not sell or share your data with anyone. The app contains no advertising or tracking libraries.

## Permissions
- Notifications, foreground service, start at boot: running timers, reminders and sync status.
- Display over other apps: only for the optional floating pill showing the running timers. Off by default.
- Ignore battery optimisation: only if you turn on background sync.
- Network and Wi-Fi state: to find and talk to your partner's phone on the local network.
- Photos / files: only when you choose to import a day image or restore a backup, using the system picker.

## Children
The app is meant for parents and caregivers, not for use by children. It stores information about a baby that you enter
yourself, only on your own devices.

## Health information
The app is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. Its estimates are general guidance based on what you log.
Talk to your paediatrician or doctor about any concern.

## Your choices
You can edit or delete any entry in the app, export everything (Settings > Data), or delete the app to remove all data.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
