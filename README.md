## Baby Tracker (Android)

A quick diary for parents and caregivers: one tap logs feeds, sleep and crying, with a day timeline, charts and gentle estimates from your own logs. No account, no ads, no analytics of ours. Everything you log stays on your phone, and two parents' phones can sync directly over their own Wi-Fi.

- Package name: `com.praxlabs.babytracker`
- [Privacy policy](privacy.md)
- [Videos](videos/): screen recordings that show how the app works (for example the foreground-service demonstrations Google Play asks for)

Baby Tracker is not medical advice. If you are worried about your baby, check with your doctor.
